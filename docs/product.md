# Product

**Flowstate is an AI co-writer that lives on a MIDI track.** You describe or play an idea. It answers with parts you hear right away, in time with your song. You shape them by talking, tweaking or touching until you drag them in.

## Core loop

1. **Context:** Flowstate knows the tempo, meter, key and what you just played.
2. **Ask:** in words, by example ("answer this riff") or with one click.
3. **Hear:** parts play through your instruments, looped, within seconds.
4. **Shape:** talk (the IR gets patched), tweak (local transforms) or touch (note edits). Full undo and branching.
5. **Commit:** drag the clip or record it into the arrangement.

## First users

Beatmakers and producers in Ableton, Logic and FL who start from chords, drums and basslines.

## v2.0 scope

- **In:** chords, bass, melody and drums (with sublanes), plus arp, pad and counter parts; host sync; capture; audition; variations; lineage; drag-out; production Q&A in the thread.
- **Out:** effect panels, mix analysis, full-song generation, audio generation, lyrics, a marketplace. Each comes back only when testers ask for it.

## Principles

- The model composes, the engine guarantees.
- Sound first: every result is audible in context within seconds.
- Edits, not re-rolls.
- The DAW is the source of truth.
- Nothing to configure before the first result.
- State never dies with a window.
- Musicality is measured.

## North star

Ideas committed to the DAW per active session. Supporting metrics: time to first sound, variations per commit, 4-week retention.
